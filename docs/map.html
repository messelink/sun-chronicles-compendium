Reading the Chart

Topology, not geography. Position implies relational role (hub, periphery, frontier), not distance.

TEC — Tinker-Evers-Chance Convergence, a rare three-way mutual beacon link. Two exist: the Chaonian core and the Trinity Coalition.

Karnos is the load-bearing junction — the one hub wiring Chaonia, Phene, the Trinity back door, and the outer rim together.

Provenance & caveats
· Every node and edge traces to the verified compendium; this map is generated from data/topology.json (still a hand-curated v0 — a blocks→JSON generator is the planned follow-up).
· Many route edges have unnamed intermediates (Yele→Chaonia 3 hops, Harahuvati→Destiny 3 drops); drawn as long-dash, not asserted as direct links.
· The unnamed 8th Mishirru core system, Karnos's dead 7th-beacon pair, and the destinations of most Yele/Anchor beacons are open questions — absent here by design.
· The Apsaras homeworld ("She Who Bore Them All") is the lost collapsed hub — off the grid, not graphed.
